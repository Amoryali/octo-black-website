Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 — Octo Black for Android (version 1.0.1).

This Privacy Policy explains how the Octo Black Android application ("the app", "we") handles information. Octo Black is a self-contained emulator frontend: it runs on your device, and your game library never leaves it unless you choose to fetch artwork.

1. Information we do NOT collect

Octo Black has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting or tracking tools. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact details (there is no sign-up).
  • Your device ID, advertising ID, phone number, or contacts.
  • Your location, camera, microphone, or call information (the app never requests these permissions).
  • Your files: ROMs, saved games, screenshots, and settings remain in the app's private storage or the folder you selected.
  • Your play history or usage statistics — nothing is telemetered.

2. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet access — used only to check for app updates and to download game cover artwork and screenshots you see in your library.
  • Network state — used only to know whether a connection is available before making those optional requests.
  • Storage / file access — used only to list and open the game folder you pick with the system file chooser. Octo Black does not read other files on your device.

3. Network requests the app makes

When the app needs artwork or checks for a newer version, it sends the following requests. Only non-personal, technical data is involved:

  • Game artwork lookup — the game title and system name are sent to our own backend (an OctoBlack Cloudflare Worker) which retrieves cover images and screenshots. That backend holds the third-party metadata credentials, so your request is not sent to the metadata provider directly and no secret is stored on your device.
  • Update check — a request to our Supabase backend asking for the latest published release number, so the app can optionally show you an "Update available" prompt. No identifiers are attached to this request.

We do not build profiles, and these requests cannot be linked to you as an individual: no cookies, no local storage identifiers, and no login tokens are used by the app.

4. Local storage on your device

Settings, library data, and downloaded artwork images are stored in the app's private storage and in Android's SharedPreferences on your device. This data is never uploaded. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes all of it.

5. Third-party services

Two services are contacted, both strictly for functionality described above, and neither receives personal information about you:

  • Supabase — serves the app's release version list (update check).
  • Cloudflare (OctoBlack Worker) — proxies game artwork requests.

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics providers, and no third-party SDKs beyond the Flutter framework itself.

6. Children's privacy

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children. Because no personal data is processed, it complies with COPPA requirements by design.

7. Your choices and control

  • You can turn off automatic artwork downloads at any time in Settings → Network & Connection.
  • You can deny network access entirely (offline mode) — the app still works; artwork and update checks are simply skipped.
  • You can delete all stored data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated with a new "Last updated" date before or at the time of the corresponding app release.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on the project repository: github.com/Amoryali/octoblackemulatorgames or use the contact links on the home page.
