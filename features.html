Advanced Features

Discover everything Octo Black brings to your Android device.

High-Performance Emulation

Pre-configured native Libretro cores including Snes9x, PCSX ReARMed, mGBA, PPSSPP, Mupen64Plus-Next, melonDS, flycast, and Stella 2023 for native 60 FPS performance.

📁

Smart Storage & Folder Watcher

Keeps your library up to date automatically when new games are added, with support for compressed archives and multi-disc games.

🎛️

Audio & Video Fine-Tuning

Custom audio muting options, aspect ratio adjustments, frame compositing, and smooth video previews.

💾

Save & Restore Game States

Never lose progress with reliable state management, instant save slots, and auto-resume features.

🎮

Custom Virtual Touch Gamepad

Fully customizable touch controls with editor, analog throttling, and physical bluetooth gamepad auto-mapping.

🛠️

Integrated CWCheat Engine

Built-in CWCheat database with manual cheat code entry support for all integrated cores.

Engineered for Stability

Designed from the ground up for full Arabic and English localizations with natural right-to-left layout transitions.
